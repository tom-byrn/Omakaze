# Code of conduct

Guided PRs adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text on that page applies to everyone who participates in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Our pledge

In short, members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation, and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

Expected behavior includes empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing others' private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers, for example through the contact details on the GitHub profile of the maintainer, [@tom-byrn](https://github.com/tom-byrn). Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter are respected.

The maintainers are responsible for enforcement and follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behavior.
