<!-- summary -->
Customers get an email with the order lines and total after checkout. Sending happens in a background job, so a slow mail server never delays the checkout response.

<!-- section: template -->
[`confirmationText`]({{diff:confirmationText}}) renders the plain-text email. Amounts use `formatMoney` with the order's currency.

<!-- section: job -->
The new [`send_order_confirmation`]({{diff:jobCase}}) job loads the order and sends the email. Orders without an email address are skipped.
