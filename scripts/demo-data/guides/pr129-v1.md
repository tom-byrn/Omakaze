<!-- summary -->
Tax was computed once on the order subtotal, while the invoice prints a rounded tax amount per line. For carts with several cheap items the printed line taxes didn't add up to the charged tax. This rounds per line, so they always do.

For three $1.99 tea towels:

| Method | Tax |
| --- | --- |
| Before: 8.25% of $5.97, rounded | $0.49 |
| After: 3 × (8.25% of $1.99, rounded) | $0.48 |

<!-- section: rounding -->
[`lineTax`]({{diff:lineTax}}) rounds one line's tax the way the invoice prints it, and [`totals`]({{diff:totalsSum}}) sums the rounded amounts.

**Check:** per-line rounding can differ from the old total by up to half a cent per line. Finance confirmed that per-line matches how the payment provider itemizes tax.
